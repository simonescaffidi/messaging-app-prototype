Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser while you visit a site. This site does not use third-party tracking cookies.

Technical cookies

We use a single technical preference (stored in your browser's localStorage, not an HTTP cookie) to remember your choice on the cookie banner. Without it, the banner would reappear on every visit.

Statistics cookies

This site currently does not use any statistical analysis tool. The "statistics" category in the banner is in place for possible future use: if a tool such as Google Analytics is ever enabled, it will only start after explicit consent.

Marketing cookies

We do not use marketing or advertising cookies or pixels.

Managing your preferences

You can clear the saved preference by clearing your browser's site data: the banner will reappear on your next visit.
