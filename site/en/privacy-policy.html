Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Who processes the data

This website and the linked prototype are a personal demonstration project. For any data-related request you can get in touch via www.simonescaffidi.it.

Data collected by the presentation website

This website does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. Only the cookie preferences you choose are saved (see Cookie Policy), locally in your browser.

Data collected by the prototype (/app)

To use the messaging prototype you create a profile with an email and a username. The following is stored on the server: email, username, public ID, access code, hidden-chat combination, added contacts, your public encryption key and the messages sent.

Messages: the content is end-to-end encrypted in the browser before sending; the server keeps only ciphertext and cannot read it. Metadata (sender, time, reactions, expiry of timed messages) remains unencrypted.

Biometric unlock: if you enable it, only the passkey's public key (WebAuthn) is stored on the server. Biometric data never leaves your device. Your private encryption key stays in the browser (localStorage).

Important: this is a demonstration prototype: email, username and access codes are stored unencrypted and the service does not meet the standards of a production product. Do not enter real sensitive information: use test data.

Purpose of processing

The data is used solely to make the demo work (authentication, messaging, contacts). It is not shared with third parties and not used for advertising profiling.

Retention

Prototype data may be deleted at any time during demo updates or resets, without notice.

Your rights

You can request deletion of the data entered in the prototype at any time by writing via the contact details above.
