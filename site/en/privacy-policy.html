Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Who processes the data

This site and the connected prototype are a personal demonstration project. For any data-related request you can reach out via www.simonescaffidi.it.

Data collected by this presentation site

This page does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools. Only your cookie preference is stored (see Cookie Policy), locally in your browser.

Data collected by the prototype (/app)

To use the messaging prototype you create a profile with an email and username. The server stores: email, username, public ID, access code, hidden-chat combination, added contacts, and the messages sent in chats.

Important: since this is a demonstration prototype, this data is stored unencrypted and is not protected to production-grade standards. Do not enter real sensitive information — use test data.

Purpose of processing

Data is used solely to run the demo (authentication, messaging, contacts). It is not shared with third parties and is not used for advertising profiling.

Retention

Prototype data may be deleted at any time during demo updates or resets, without notice.

Your rights

You can request deletion of the data you entered in the prototype at any time via the contact above.
